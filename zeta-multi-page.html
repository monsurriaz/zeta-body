Why these forms matter

Methylated B-vitamins skip the conversion step — people with the MTHFR variant (30–40% of the population) actually absorb them. Albion® chelated minerals are bound to amino acids — far better absorbed than cheap oxide forms. Quatrefolic® folate is already in the bioactive 5-MTHF form your body uses directly.

How to take it.

Take 2 capsules once daily with a meal. Fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) absorb better with some dietary fat.

Best Time

With your largest meal of the day. Morning or midday works well — B-vitamins can be mildly energizing.
