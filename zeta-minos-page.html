This complete profile is specifically designed to trigger muscle protein synthesis (MPS), fight sarcopenia, and support lifelong strength and vitality.

Why we added L-Arginine — the smart advantage

Most EAA formulas stop at the basics. ZETA MINOS goes further by including L-Arginine, a strategic choice backed by science:

  • Acts as a precursor to nitric oxide — improving blood flow, oxygen, and nutrient delivery to working muscles
  • Supports better muscle perfusion and recovery — especially important as we age
  • Studies on EAA + Arginine supplementation in older adults showed meaningful gains in lean body mass, strength, and physical function — benefits beyond EAAs alone

The research underneath.

Two pieces of the MINOS science deserve highlighting: (1) why we added L-Arginine (Børsheim 2008) and (2) why we dose leucine high in older adults (Katsanos 2006). Plus the broader literature behind EAA supplementation for muscle and longevity.

★ Key study · the EAA + Arginine paper

Børsheim E, Bui Q-UT, Tissier S, Kobayashi H, Ferrando AA, Wolfe RR (2008). Effect of amino acid supplementation on muscle mass, strength and physical function in elderly. Clinical Nutrition.

16-week double-blind RCT in glucose-intolerant elderly. EAA + Arginine supplementation between meals significantly increased lean body mass (+1.14 kg at 12 weeks), lower-extremity strength (+22%), and physical function including gait speed and mobility.
