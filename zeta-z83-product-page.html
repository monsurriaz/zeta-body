Other Ingredients

Hypromellose (vegetarian capsule), organic rice extract blend.

Why these forms

Amatea Max is a clinically standardized guayusa extract — not just dried tea leaves. It carries L-theanine analogs and antioxidant polyphenols alongside its natural caffeine.

Zembrin is the patented Sceletium extract with multiple human clinical trials behind it (mood, focus, anxiety markers). Generic Sceletium powders don't have this.

Caffeine from organic green coffee bean keeps the source clean — no synthetic stimulants, no mycotoxins.

How to take it.

Take 1–2 capsules daily, in the morning, with or without food. Most people feel the lift within 30 minutes. Don't take more than 3 a day.

Best Time

Morning. Avoid taking after 3pm — even small doses of caffeine can affect sleep for sensitive folks.

Pairs Well With

  • ZETA MULTI — the daily Foundation of vitamins and minerals
  • ZETA D+K — the longevity foundation
  • ZETA MINOS — strength + recovery on workout days

Precautions

Contains caffeine. If you're sensitive, start with 1 capsule to assess tolerance. Avoid combining with other caffeine sources for your first dose. If pregnant, nursing, under 18, or on medication, consult your healthcare provider before use. Don't exceed 3 capsules in a day.
