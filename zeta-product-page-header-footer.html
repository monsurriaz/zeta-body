★ Key publication · neuroprotection & sport

Kreider RB, Kalman DS, Antonio J, Almada AL, et al. (2017) — International Society of Sports Nutrition position stand: safety and efficacy of creatine supplementation in exercise, sport, and medicine. Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition. 14:18. The landmark ISSN position paper co-authored by Anthony Almada establishing creatine's neuroprotective properties, including concussion protection, spinal cord neuroprotection, and applications in neurodegenerative disease.

Kreider RB, Stout JR. (2021) — Creatine in Health and Disease. Nutrients. 13(2):447. Comprehensive review of creatine's role in cellular energy, muscle performance, and systemic health.

Prokopidis K et al. (2023) — Effects of creatine supplementation on memory in healthy individuals: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Nutrition Reviews. 81(4):416–427. Meta-analysis of 8 randomized trials; creatine enhanced memory performance in adults regardless of dose, sex, or location.

Xu C et al. (2024) — Creatine and cognitive performance: meta-analysis of 16 randomized trials. Frontiers in Nutrition. 11:1424972. Beneficial cognitive effects in memory, attention, and processing speed across ages 20–76.

Delpino FM et al. (2022) — Creatine supplementation and lean body mass. Nutrition. 103–104:111791. Meta-analysis confirming creatine combined with resistance exercise increases lean body mass regardless of age.

Antonio J et al. (2025) — Common questions and misconceptions about creatine supplementation. Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition. 22(1):2441760. Updated position on safety, dosing, and broad applications.
