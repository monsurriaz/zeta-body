Most Studied

The Proven Anchor

B. lactis HN019®

HOWARU™ certified · most-studied strain in the formula

The anchor of this formula. In a randomized controlled trial of 100 adults, daily HN019® supplementation significantly reduced whole-gut transit time within 14 days — at both high and low doses. It adheres to epithelial cells, survives GI transit intact, and has a safety record spanning decades of published human research. The foundation every other strain is built around.

The Human Native

L. acidophilus La-14

of human origin · type A1 L. acidophilus

Found naturally in the human intestinal tract, mouth, and vagina — and in fermented milks like kefir for centuries. The La-14 strain is of human origin, which matters: strains that evolved alongside human biology tend to adhere better and colonize more effectively. Demonstrates excellent adhesion to human epithelial cell lines. It belongs here.

The Fermented Foods Strain

L. plantarum Lp-115

from plant origin · olives, sauerkraut, kimchi

Found in lactic acid-fermented foods across cultures — olives, sauerkraut, kimchi — for centuries before we understood why. The Lp-115 strain is tough in the right way: resistant to low pH conditions, bile-tolerant at duodenal concentrations, and demonstrates excellent adhesion to epithelial cell lines. It doesn't just survive the GI tract. It's built for it.

The Intestinal Specialist

B. longum Bl-05

Bl-05 strain · intestinal environment native

Well-suited to the intestinal environment — which sounds simple, but it's not. B. longum Bl-05 is resistant to both low pH and bile salts, meaning it makes it to the target tissue alive and ready. Well-established safety profile, documented performance in the gut. The quiet, reliable fourth member of this stack.

For the Curious

Dig deeper.

The probiotic science, full ingredients, dosing notes, and the research behind the four strains.

ZETA PRO-B is a vegetarian, dairy-free, gluten-free four-strain probiotic delivering 100 billion CFU per capsule. The four strains — B. lactis HN019®, L. acidophilus La-14, L. plantarum Lp-115, and B. longum Bl-05 — are selected for their published research, ability to survive GI transit, and demonstrated adhesion to intestinal epithelial cells. PRO-B uses DRcaps™ gastro-resistant capsules to protect live organisms from stomach acid and ensure targeted release in the small intestine. Each capsule is individually sealed in nitrogen-purged aluminum blister packs to protect against heat, moisture, and oxygen — from the first capsule to the last. One capsule daily.

  • Bifidobacterium lactis HN019® (HOWARU™) — The flagship strain. Extensively studied in human RCTs for bowel regularity, GI transit time, and microbiome diversity. Survives GI transit, adheres to epithelial cells, has a 20+ year safety record in published literature.
  • Lactobacillus acidophilus La-14 — Human-origin strain, classified as type A1 L. acidophilus. Demonstrates excellent adhesion to human epithelial cell lines. Historically present in fermented milks and naturally occurring in human GI flora.
  • Lactobacillus plantarum Lp-115 — Isolated from plant material; abundant in lactic acid-fermented foods. Demonstrates excellent epithelial adhesion in vitro. Resistant to low pH and survives bile at duodenal concentrations.
  • Bifidobacterium longum Bl-05 — Well-established safety profile. Naturally resistant to low pH and bile salts. Well-suited to the intestinal environment with documented performance in the gut.
  • Capsule: DRcaps™ vegetarian capsule (hypromellose). No additional fillers, binders, or additives.

Take 1 capsule daily, preferably in the morning. The DRcaps™ capsule handles stomach transit — no need to adjust timing around meals. Consistency matters more than perfect timing. Store at room temperature, away from heat and moisture. Do not refrigerate (unnecessary with nitrogen-purged blister pack storage).

Precautions: Probiotics are generally well-tolerated. Some individuals experience mild GI adjustment in the first 1–2 weeks as the microbiome shifts — this is normal. If you are immunocompromised, critically ill, or post-surgical, check with your physician before use. If pregnant or nursing, check with your provider. Not intended for use in infants without medical guidance.

Waller PA et al. Dose-response effect of Bifidobacterium lactis HN019 on whole gut transit time and functional gastrointestinal symptoms in adults. Scandinavian Journal of Gastroenterology (2011). 100-subject RCT demonstrating statistically significant reduction in GI transit time with daily HN019® supplementation. The clinical anchor for the transit-time claim — and the study that set HN019® apart from other strains.

Vanderpool C, Yan F, Polk DB. Mechanisms of probiotic action: Implications for therapeutic applications in inflammatory bowel diseases. Inflammatory Bowel Diseases (2008). Comprehensive review of the seven mechanisms by which probiotics benefit health — metabolic activity, epithelial adhesion, microbial balance, immune function, and more. The scientific framework behind the multi-strain approach.

Ahmed M et al. Impact of Bifidobacterium lactis HN019 on intestinal microflora of elderly subjects. Journal of Nutrition Health & Aging (2007). Statistically significant increases in beneficial microflora in subjects over 60 — the demographic where microbiome diversity declines most sharply and supplementation matters most.

Gopal PK et al. Effects of Bifidobacterium lactis HN019 and galacto-oligosaccharides on gastrointestinal microflora in human subjects. Nutrition Research (2003). Documents measurable increases in bifidobacteria and lactobacilli following HN019® supplementation — the microbiome population shift that drives downstream benefits.

Collado MC, Meriluoto J, Salminen S. Role of commercial probiotic strains against human pathogen adhesion to intestinal mucus. Letters in Applied Microbiology (2007). Establishes competitive adhesion properties of L. plantarum Lp-115 and L. acidophilus La-14 — the mechanism by which beneficial strains displace less desirable organisms from intestinal tissue.
