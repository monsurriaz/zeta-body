Two vitamins, one capsule — done right.

D+K 5000 is a complex of vitamin K2 and vitamin D3. It delivers vitamin K2 as menaquinone-7 (MK-7) — the most bioavailable and bioactive form of K2 — alongside vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol), the identical form your body synthesizes when sunlight hits your skin.

Historical use and decades of clinical research support the role of vitamin K supplementation in bone and cardiovascular health. Vitamin D3 supports immune function, mood, hormone balance, and bone health. Together, they make the daily-foundation duo most adults are quietly short on.

Clinical Applications

  • Supports bone health by promoting carboxylation of bone proteins
  • Supports cardiovascular health by influencing arterial calcium deposits
  • Supports healthy blood clotting
  • Supports immune function
  • Supports calcium absorption and utilization

Other Ingredients

Hypromellose (vegetarian capsule), microcrystalline cellulose, medium-chain triglycerides, silica.

Why these forms

MK-7 has a half-life of about three days in the body — much longer than other K2 forms (MK-4 lasts about an hour). That means one daily capsule maintains steady levels.

D3 (cholecalciferol) is the same molecule your skin makes from sunlight — far more bioavailable than D2.

How to take it.

Take 1 capsule daily, with a meal that has some fat — eggs, avocado, olive oil, yogurt, whatever you eat. D3 and K2 are fat-soluble; they absorb best with food.

Best Time

Morning or midday works best for most people. D3 can be mildly energizing — so we don't recommend it right before sleep.

Precautions

If you're on blood thinners (warfarin, Coumadin), check with your physician before starting D+K — vitamin K can interact with these medications. If pregnant, nursing, or under 18, consult your healthcare provider. Don't exceed the recommended serving.
