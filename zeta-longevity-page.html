The Sirtuin Switch

Resveratrol

from Polygonum cuspidatum · 75 mg per capsule

The famous one. The "Mediterranean diet" molecule that scientists have been studying since the '90s. Activates sirtuins — the proteins your cells use to defend and repair themselves. The science isn't hype; it's just slow, the way real longevity science tends to be.

The Senolytic

Quercetin

from Sophora japonica bud · 250 mg per capsule

The senolytic. One of the ways cells age badly is by accumulating senescent cells — cells that have stopped functioning but refuse to die, lingering in tissue and generating chronic low-grade inflammation. Scientists sometimes call them zombie cells: not quite alive, not quite gone, and actively dragging down the healthy cells around them. Quercetin is one of the best-studied compounds for clearing them out. It also synergizes with the other two, which is why stacking matters.

The Methylated Upgrade

Pterostilbene

PteroPure® (ChromaDex) · 62.5 mg per capsule

Methylated resveratrol. The methyl groups cap resveratrol's free hydroxyl sites — the result is dramatically higher hepatic stability, better intestinal absorption, and a longer half-life in circulation. Your body gets more of it, and it stays longer. We use PteroPure® — the branded, pharmaceutical-grade form from ChromaDex, the same company behind NR research.

What's in it.

Active Ingredients

  • Quercetin Dihydrate (from Sophora japonica bud) — 250 mg. Senolytic bioflavonoid. Supports clearance of senescent cells and has a demonstrated anti-inflammatory profile, including RCT-level CRP reduction data.
  • trans-Resveratrol (from Polygonum cuspidatum root extract) — 75 mg. Sirtuin activator. The most-studied polyphenol in longevity research.
  • trans-Pterostilbene (PteroPure® — ChromaDex) — 62.5 mg. Methylated resveratrol analog. ~7× more bioavailable, longer plasma half-life, and superior cellular uptake versus resveratrol alone.

Other Ingredients

Capsule (hypromellose and water), microcrystalline cellulose, stearic acid, magnesium stearate, silica, medium-chain triglyceride oil. MCT oil aids fat-soluble absorption — no need to take with a fatty meal.

How to take it.

Take 1 capsule twice daily. The formula includes MCT oil — fat-soluble absorption is built in. Take with or without food. Morning and evening is the simplest pattern. Any consistent schedule works.

Precautions

Pregnant or nursing? Check with your provider. Quercetin can interact with certain antibiotics (especially fluoroquinolones) — worth a conversation with your physician if relevant. If you're on blood thinners, resveratrol at higher doses may have mild antiplatelet effects; discuss with your doctor.

The evidence.

Baur JA, Sinclair DA. Therapeutic potential of resveratrol: the in vivo evidence. Nature Reviews Drug Discovery (2006). The paper that put resveratrol on the map — sirtuin activation, caloric restriction mimicry, and the longevity pathway case made rigorously.
Hsu YH et al. Effect of pterostilbene on glucose and lipid-related markers in a randomized controlled trial. Nutrition Journal (2012). Human RCT showing pterostilbene's metabolic effects — one of the cleaner clinical demonstrations in this space.
Riche DM et al. Analysis of safety from a human clinical trial with pterostilbene. Journal of Toxicology (2013). Establishes the safety profile at doses relevant to supplementation. Pterostilbene was well-tolerated.
Hickson LJ et al. Senolytics decrease senescent cells in humans: preliminary report from a clinical trial of dasatinib plus quercetin in individuals with diabetic kidney disease. EBioMedicine (2019). First-in-human senolytic trial — the quercetin study that legitimized the senescence clearance angle.
Serban MC et al. Effects of quercetin on glycaemic control in patients with type 2 diabetes. Nutrition (2016). Pooled RCT analysis demonstrating quercetin's significant reduction in circulating CRP.
