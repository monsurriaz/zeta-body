For the curious

Dig deeper.

The MaxSimil absorption science, full ingredients, dosing notes, and the research behind EPA + DHA.

The ZETA MEGAS formula features MaxSimil® monoglyceride fish oil, which delivers a three times greater EPA + DHA absorption rate than equivalent doses of standard fish oils. Through MaxSimil's patented PLATform (Pre-digested Lipid Absorption Technology), omega-3s are delivered in their pre-processed monoglyceride form — bypassing the digestion step that limits absorption of triglyceride and ethyl ester fish oils.

Each daily serving delivers 1,300 mg of high-purity omega-3s with the EPA + DHA ratio your body uses for inflammation management, brain health, cardiovascular support, and skin.*

  • Supports cardiovascular health — triglycerides, blood pressure, vessel function
  • Supports brain function — cognition, mood, memory
  • Supports healthy inflammatory response
  • Supports joint comfort and mobility
  • Supports skin elasticity and barrier function
  • Supports eye health (DHA is concentrated in the retina)

MaxSimil® Monoglyceride Fish Oil — 1,300 mg per serving (2 softgels).

  • EPA (Eicosapentaenoic acid) — ~715 mg
  • DHA (Docosahexaenoic acid) — ~410 mg
  • Other omega-3 fatty acids — remainder

Other Ingredients: Bovine gelatin (softgel), glycerin, water, natural lemon flavor, mixed tocopherols (antioxidant).

Sourcing + Purity: Sustainably sourced from small wild-caught cold-water fish (anchovy, sardine, mackerel). Third-party tested for heavy metals, PCBs, and oxidation. Below all international standards for omega-3 purity.

Why this form: Triglyceride and ethyl ester fish oils need lipase enzymes to break them down before absorption. MaxSimil delivers omega-3s in monoglyceride form — the form your gut would produce mid-digestion anyway — skipping the bottleneck. Up to 3× more EPA and DHA make it into your bloodstream.

Take 2 softgels daily. Most people take both in the morning. Unlike standard fish oils, MaxSimil absorbs well with or without food — you can take it on an empty stomach if that fits your routine.

Pairs well with: ZETA D+K (both fat-soluble; they support each other's absorption), ZETA MULTI (the daily foundation), ZETA LONGEVITY (for the long-game antioxidant stack).

Precautions: If you're on blood thinners (warfarin, aspirin, Plavix), check with your physician — omega-3s at high doses can affect clotting. If pregnant, nursing, or have a fish/shellfish allergy, consult your healthcare provider. Don't exceed 2 softgels per day.
